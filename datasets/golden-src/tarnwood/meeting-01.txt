Rucheek: Okay, so the stat key itself is essentially the column descriptor. And the data points are time series - timestamp plus value pairs?

Priya Nair: Exactly. And the value is usually a numeric metric, could be a percentage, could be KB, could be milliseconds depending on the stat key. The unit isn't always explicit in the response, it depends on the endpoint version.

Rucheek: That's actually really useful to know early. I'll probably need to build a stat key vocabulary or at least prompt the LLM with enough context about VMware naming conventions.
